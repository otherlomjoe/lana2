Woodland Dream

Description

Acrylic

This acrylic painting on canvas started as a simple study of a girl with a beautiful dress and acquired an intriguing life of its own. As I painted her, I realised I really wanted to have her interact with something and the Australian Eastern Water Dragon was the perfect size and attitude to converse with her. I loved the light on her and the contrasting dark of the woodland around her and instead of a super tight, realistic painting that I first intended I ended up keeping most of the initial looseness of the brushstrokes that I marked the scene out with. This has ended up being one of my fantasy works, in keeping with the mermaids and dragons (mythological) that I enjoy doing. To me she seems like a fairy or something otherworldly that has taken on a human appearance, perhaps because she seems to glow.

My source material was a photo by Howard Lyon (model Tessa Halterman) and the dragon was from my personal collection of photos of Prince Stripe in my backyard.
